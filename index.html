Our Network

Current Programmes & Institutions

A diverse family of educational and community programmes, each committed to the highest standards of learning and moral development.

📿
Islamic Studies
C. Usthad Memorial Dars
A traditional dars offering classical Islamic sciences in memory of the late C. Usthad, nurturing scholars rooted in authentic scholarship.
🧠
Academic Excellence
IQ Academy
A centre of intellectual development focused on enhancing academic performance, critical thinking, and conceptual clarity for students.
📖
Quranic Education
Hifzul Quran
A dedicated Quran memorisation programme providing students with the guidance and environment to achieve complete memorisation of the Holy Quran.
🌟
Early Childhood
ZeeQue Pre-School
A stimulating early childhood education programme nurturing the youngest minds with a value-based, play-centred learning approach.
🏫
Islamic School
Hi-Tech School Madrasa
A modern madrasa blending traditional Islamic education with contemporary teaching methods and digital learning tools.
🎓
Youth Development
Youth School
Empowering young men and women with leadership skills, moral grounding, and academic excellence to shape the next generation of leaders.
📱
Digital Literacy
Girls Model Academy
A pioneering programme providing girls with digital literacy, moral values, and online safety awareness in a supportive environment.
💻
Online Learning
Online & Reading Room
A well-resourced reading room and online learning space open to students and the community for self-directed study and digital education.
🤲
Welfare
Relief Fund
A structured relief fund extending financial assistance to families and individuals in need, ensuring no one is left behind in times of hardship.
🕌
Community Centre
Juma Masjid
The central masjid serving as the spiritual heart of the community — a place of worship, learning, and community gathering.
🎤
Annual Event
Annual Gathering (Jalsa)
An annual convention bringing together scholars, students, and the wider community to celebrate knowledge, honour achievements, and renew purpose.
🌺
Spiritual Programme
Soofna Centre
A dedicated centre for spiritual growth, mindfulness, and moral enrichment, supporting the inner development of students and community members.

Food Welfare Programmes

Scholarship Programmes
